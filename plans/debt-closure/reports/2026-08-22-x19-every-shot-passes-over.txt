       head   HIT  t=[5.63, 5.85]

WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG
----------------------
The client's rendered body intermittently sits exactly **0.332 m below** the server's
authoritative position. Transform vs snapshot, every checkpoint, three runs, all three
clients:

    x18-fixed      driver     spawned    -0.332
                   driver     in-range    0.000
                   observer-a firing     -0.678   (= 2 x 0.339)
                   observer-a victim-... -0.332
                   observer-b killed     -0.332
    x19-measure    observer-a approach   -0.332
                   observer-a firing     -0.332
                   observer-b spawned    -0.332
                   observer-b in-range   -0.345
    x19-occlusion  driver     firing     -0.332

The value is quantised, not drift: it is 0 or -0.332, once exactly twice that. It lands
on any of the three clients and flickers between samples, which is what an oscillation
looks like when you sample it seven times.

**One mechanism, both runs.** Whichever side is sampled low when the trigger is pulled,
the ray ends up above the boxes:

  - x19-occlusion: the SHOOTER was 0.332 low. Re-running the slab test with the server's
    own origin (y=36.0, the authoritative eye) instead of the client's rendered eye:

        head MISS   torso MISS   arms MISS   legs MISS
        at 5.9 m the ray is at y=36.41; the head box spans 36.05..36.29

  - x19-measure: the shooter matched, so the TARGET's rewound pose must be the low one.
    Applying the same -0.332 to the victim's boxes:

        present pose:        head HIT  t=[5.63, 5.85]
        pose 0.332 lower:    head MISS t=[6.78, 5.94]

    The identical offset flips the one run that the shooter-side explanation could not
    account for. That is why this is one defect and not two.

The client aims from the body it DRAWS; the server shoots from the position it HOLDS.
While those disagree by a third of a metre, a player aiming centre-of-head at six metres
misses over the top — and the harness inherits exactly the same error, because aiming at
what is drawn is what a human does.


WHAT 0.332 IS, IS NOT KNOWN
---------------------------
Ruled out: the stance change (StandHeight 1.8 -> CrouchHeight 0.5 is a 1.3 m step, and
half of it through an unmoved CharacterController.center would be 0.65). Not the
position quantum either (POS_RANGE 4096 / 65535 = 0.0625 m; 0.332 is not a multiple).

DO NOT guess the constant. The next measurement is named and cheap: the shot log prints
`nearest[... torso=...]` from `target.Present`, but the resolver used `frame.Boxes` from
the rewound tick. Print BOTH, plus the frame's tick and the shooter's own
`Movement.State.Position` beside `transform.position`. That separates "the pose was
recorded low" from "the pose was recorded fine and the body is drawn low" — which are
different files.
