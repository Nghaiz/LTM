3F.2 — ROOT CAUSE
------------------
A second run (artifacts/lane-b/x19-move) adds one line per simulated client tick
(NetMovementAgent.LogTick) and one per correction (`[move-correct]`). The sawtooth is
visible directly, on all three clients, for the whole run:

    [move-correct] pre=31.2917 wanted=31.9692 post=31.9692   <- correction lands
    [move]  pre=31.9692 asked=-0.3322 granted=-0.3322 post=31.6370  velY=-9.967  ctrl=False
    [move]  pre=31.6370 asked=-0.3453 granted=-0.3453 post=31.2917  velY=-10.360 ctrl=False
    [move-correct] pre=31.2917 wanted=31.9692 post=31.9692   <- correction lands again

`granted == asked` exactly. Nothing absorbed the motion.

    ctrl=False        on 11,785 of 11,785 client ticks, all three clients, whole run
    grounded=False    on every one of them
    flags=None        on every one of them

THE LINE
    Ironfront_Reborn/Assets/Scripts/Net/Shared/NetMovementAgent.cs, CharacterMove:

        if (_controller != null && _controller.enabled)
            LastCollisionFlags = _controller.Move(delta);
        else
            transform.position += delta;      <-- HERE
        return transform.position;

    On a client the CharacterController is disabled, so every predicted tick takes the
    `else` branch: the motion is written straight onto the transform, no sweep, no floor,
    no collision flags -- and the method then returns `transform.position` as though the
    move had been resolved. `Tick` stores that as `State.Position`, so the client's own
    record of where it is agrees with a position collision never validated. It is a
    silent fallback of exactly the shape development-principles.md forbids.

WHY THE CONTROLLER IS DISABLED
    FpsActorController.Start() (Assets/Scripts/Assembly-CSharp/FpsActorController.cs:192)
    calls DisableInput(), and DisableInput() (:326) sets `characterController.enabled =
    false`. The ONLY thing that re-enables it is EnableInput(), and the only caller on a
    live body is FpsActorController.SpawnAt(:425) -- the gameplay spawn a networked body
    deliberately never runs (Actor.cs:270, "Deliberately NOT SpawnAt, and deliberately not
    controller.EnableInput()"). So on a networked client the flag is cleared at Start and
    never set again: not once in 11,785 ticks.

WHY IT NEVER RE-GROUNDS
    NetMovementAgent.IsGrounded is `_controller.isGrounded`, which is false for a disabled
    controller. So MovementCore.Step takes the AIRBORNE branch on the client forever, and
    the grounded branch -- the one that would pin the body to the floor -- is unreachable.

THE MAGNITUDE, DERIVED
    1. The SERVER is grounded, so MovementCore.Step sets its velocity.Y to
       -StickToGroundForce = -10.0 m/s (MovementCore.cs:83).
    2. The wire is an i8. Quantize.PackVel(-10) = (sbyte)(-10 * 127/64) = (sbyte)(-19.84375)
       = -19; UnpackVel(-19) = -19 / (127/64) = -9.5748031496063 m/s.
    3. ClientPredictionStage.OnSnapshotApplied copies that into the state the client
       predicts from, and PredictionReconciler.Reconcile's replay runs MovementCore.Step
       once -- airborne, per above -- adding one tick of gravity:
           -9.5748031496063 + (-9.81 * 1.2 / 30) = -9.9672031496063 m/s
    4. The next predicted tick asks for velocity.Y * dt, dt = 1/30:
           -9.9672031496063 / 30 = -0.33224010 m

    measured, checkpoint:  -0.33224110      derived: -0.33224010   (1.0e-6, float32)
    measured, [move] line: asked=-0.3322    velY=-9.967

    A second tick before the next correction:
           -9.9672031496063 - 11.772/30 = -10.3596 -> /30 = -0.34532
    measured: asked=-0.3453, velY=-10.360, and dY=-0.34532 at three checkpoints.

    Both ticks together:  0.33224 + 0.34532 = 0.67756
    measured: dY=-0.67756 (x19-move, observer-a in-range); the 2026-08-22 report's -0.678.

    Every value the two reports observed -- 0, -0.332, -0.345, -0.678 -- is accounted for.
    Nothing here is a plausible arithmetic story: each number comes from a named constant
    or a named line, and the log prints the intermediate velocity that connects them.

WHY IT OSCILLATES RATHER THAN DRIFTING
    The body falls 0.332 m per predicted tick and the next snapshot correction
    (ApplyCorrectedState, hardSnap=false) puts it back on the authoritative position. The
    fall and the correction are the two halves of a sawtooth at snapshot rate. A checkpoint
    samples it 0, 1 or 2 ticks after the last correction, which is why the record shows
    exactly three values and never anything between them. That is also why it looked
    intermittent and looked like it landed on "any of the three clients": it is happening
    to all three, continuously.

WHY EVERY SHOT PASSED OVER
    The client aims from the body it draws, which is up to 0.678 m below the body the
    server holds. The server's ShotOrigin adds EYE_HEIGHT to its own (higher) position and
    fires along the pitch the client computed from its own (lower) one. At six metres a
    third of a metre is the whole head box -- which is exactly the slab test in the
    2026-08-22 report, from both ends.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
    Not the stance change: height is 1.800 and centerY 0.000 on every logged tick, crouch
    False throughout. Not the position quantum: the client's y is unquantised and the two
    sides differ by a velocity integral, not a rounding step. Both were correctly ruled out
    on 2026-08-22 and neither is revived here.


A SECOND DEFECT, FOUND AND NOT FIXED HERE
------------------------------------------
PredictionReconciler.Reconcile replays unacknowledged inputs with

    MovementCore.Step(ref predicted, in _inputs[slot], dt);

and discards the return value. MovementCore.Step's own remark says Position is "not"
written -- "only the collision system knows where the actor really ended up, so the caller
writes it back after moving". So the replay advances velocity and stance but NEVER the
position: every correction lands the client on the server's STALE position with the
unacknowledged motion thrown away. It is why `corrections` reaches 2208 in a 136 s run
without ever converging, and why `pendingInputs` sits pinned at Capacity.

It is visible in the same artifacts and is NOT this row. Filed as X-20 rather than fixed
here, per phase-3f section 6 outcome 2 and AC-6.
