2. THE FLAKE RATE DID NOT MOVE, AND THAT IS THE FINDING
--------------------------------------------------------

Check 1 is still 1 of 3. With the weapon pinned and the witness mostly out of the way, the
cause is now visible with nothing else on top of it:

    run   min distance   occluded   of which the victim's own bone   hits   outcome
    01        3.30 m         0                    0                   4     KILL
    02        1.52 m        16                   16                   0     nothing
    03        1.93 m        18                   18                   0     nothing

**Every occlusion in all three runs is `collider=Bone_002 layer=8`, at `frac=0.938..0.960`.**
Not one is terrain, not one is a building. `frac` near 1.0 puts the blocker at the endpoint
-- inside the victim it is supposed to have hit -- and the occlusion mask `-2049` excludes
only layer 11, so layer 8 blocks.

That is X-26, and this is a far stronger statement of it than the row was filed with. The
original observation (12 of 30 at ~1 m, in a run whose weapon and witness were both
uncontrolled) could not rule out the weapon or the witness. This one can: same weapon, same
programme, same spawn slot, and the ONLY run that scored is the only run where the pair
never got inside 3.3 m.

**So X-26 is distance-dependent and it is check 1's flake.** Not the aim (X-25, closed),
not the loadout (X-27, closed), not the witness (X-28, mostly), and not the spawn (X-22,
closed).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. Three runs are three runs. 3.3 m is one clean sample above the
threshold and 1.52/1.93 m are two below it; the crossover is somewhere in between and this
does not locate it. It also does not say the bone SHOULD be excluded -- widening the mask is
a game decision with its own consequences for shooting through bodies, and X-26 belongs to
phase 6 for exactly that reason.


3. WHY THE PAIR IS THAT CLOSE AT ALL
-------------------------------------

`combat-driver.json` sets `holdDistanceMeters: 6.0`, which reads as "close to 6 m". It never
runs: with the spawn pinned, all three bodies start INSIDE 6 m of each other, so
`ApproachMoveZ` returns 0 from the first frame and the driver never moves. The 1.5..3.3 m
spread across runs is spawn jitter at the shared point, not an approach.

This is the cost of X-22's pin stated precisely, and it is why X-28's remaining half matters
more than it looked: the pin does not merely co-locate the witness, it puts the SHOOTER
inside the range where X-26 fires. A programme that backed the driver off to 5-6 m before
firing would probably score every run -- and would be measuring around a game defect rather
than through it, which is a decision for whoever picks up X-26, not a thing to slip into the
harness quietly.
