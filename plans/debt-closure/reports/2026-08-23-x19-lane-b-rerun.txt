AND THE REPLAY THAT NEVER MOVES — FILED AS X-21
------------------------------------------------
`PredictionReconciler.Reconcile` replays unacknowledged inputs with
`MovementCore.Step(ref predicted, ...)` and discards the return value, while `Step`'s own
remark says it does not write `Position`. So replay advances velocity and stance and
never the position: a correction lands the client on the server's stale position with the
unacknowledged motion thrown away. Found statically, corroborated by the pre-fix
`corrections: 2208` that never converged. The fix dropped that to 0 on the driver by
removing what it was correcting, so the fault is now quiet rather than gone. Filed as
X-21.
