What this falsifies, from debt-ledger.md X-13:
  * 'whether OnSpawnActor applies that position to the LOCAL actor' -- not the question.
    The snapshot path already carries it: inSnapshot=true and the authoritative position
    is the server's own, decoded, at every checkpoint.
  * 'correctionSnaps: 0, correctionBlends: 0, lastPositionErrorM: 0.00' -- those three are
    VEHICLE-scoped (ClientVehicleStage.DrivenStats) and read zero on any on-foot programme
    regardless. The actor reconciler ran 901 times in this run.

Root cause: NetMovementAgent.ApplyAuthoritativeState sets the MoveState struct and calls
ApplyStanceHeight, and never touches the transform. Its own remarks say why that is correct
-- for the SERVER, where InputAuthority.ApplyPendingInput has already driven the
CharacterController: 'harmless for position, which the CharacterController already moved'.
ClientPredictionStage.OnSnapshotApplied calls the same method with the opposite precondition.
So 901 corrections were computed, accepted and dropped, and the body free-fell from y=996.6
to y=970.8 while the server held it at (1603.1, 42.3, 1437.7).
