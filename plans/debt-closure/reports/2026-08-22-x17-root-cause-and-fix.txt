X-17 — a remote player's proxy is spawned and never positioned
==============================================================
2026-08-22. Runs: artifacts/lane-b/x17-measure-01, artifacts/lane-b/x17-fix-culled.
Checkpoints preserved under plans/debt-closure/reports/lane-b/.


WHAT THE PREVIOUS ROW SAID, AND WHY IT WAS WRONG
------------------------------------------------
The ledger read X-17 as "the combat programme cannot reach its own victim: OBS-A is
2139 m away ... a harness problem, not an engine one". That is wrong twice over.

2139 m is not a distance between two players. The six spawn points on Dustbowl are at
most 1173 m apart:

    0 (1086, 954)   1 (1150, 1340)  2 (1895, 1804)
    3 (2085, 1140)  4 (1202, 1768)  5 (1610, 1437)

No pair of them is 2139 m apart, and two clients have landed on the SAME point in a
previous run. The number matched something else entirely.


THE MEASUREMENT
---------------
Added to LaneBCheckpointRecorder: for the actor the step is aiming at, the PROXY's
transform position on this client, whether the actor is in the decoded snapshot, and
the position that snapshot carries. Two fields, because they separate the only two
causes there are.

x17-measure-01, driver's seven checkpoints:

    proxyX/Y/Z    = (0, 2000, 0)          at ALL seven, unchanging
    inSnapshot    = false at five, true at in-range and firing
    snapshotX/Y/Z = (1088.11, 103.41, 954.30)   when present
    OBS-A's own record            (1088.1,  103.1,  954.3)
    server log, actor 42 placed at (1088.15, 103.47, 954.30)

So the wire is correct to the centimetre. Interest culling, the delta decoder and the
transport are all doing their jobs. The proxy transform was simply never written.

A TRAP WORTH RECORDING: reading this off `pitch` alone gives the opposite answer.
ScriptedAim's pitch is POSITIVE-IS-DOWN (its class remark says so, and cites
ServerCombatAuthority.AimDirection). The block carried pitch = -42.6, which under the
ordinary sign convention puts the target 1934 m BELOW the map — a body falling out of
the world, which is exactly the X-15 failure and exactly what this looked like at first
reading. Under the real convention it is 1972 m ABOVE the map. Same number, opposite
diagnosis. This is why the instrumentation reports coordinates and not an angle.


ROOT CAUSE
----------
Three shipped behaviours, each correct alone:

 1. ServerTickLoop.AnnounceNewActors announces EVERY actor to every client, regardless
    of interest, and MarkSpawnSent guarantees it is sent exactly once.
 2. InterestManager REMOVES an actor past CullRadius (500 m) from the accumulated world,
    so it disappears from DeltaDecoder.Current.
 3. SnapshotInterpolator.TryLerpPosition needs the actor in BOTH endpoints, so an actor
    that is intermittently or never present is never lerped.

Together they describe an actor that is announced and then never replicated. For that
actor S_SPAWN_ACTOR carries the only position it will ever be given — and
RemoteActorRegistry.OnSpawn threw it away, popping a pooled transform and calling
SetActive(true) without ever placing it.

The consequence is not cosmetic. Anything reading that transform gets a confident wrong
answer: the scripted solver reported `resolved: true` at 2570 m and fired 240 rounds
into open sky. A human's crosshair would have done the same.


THE FIX, AND WHAT IT DID NOT FIX
--------------------------------
RemoteActorRegistry.OnSpawn now places the transform from message.PosX/PosY/PosZ and
message.Yaw.

x17-fix-culled, driver's checkpoints:

    proxyX/Y/Z = (0.03125, 999.984, 0.03125)     was (0, 2000, 0)

The proxy MOVED, so the placement runs. It moved to the wrong place, and that is a
SECOND defect this uncovered rather than a failure of the fix:

    server: actor 42 placed at spawn point 2 of 6 (1885.33, 26.46, 1805.13)
    proxy:                                        (0.03,   999.98, 0.03)

IronfrontNetBindings.CreatePlayerBody does `Instantiate(manager.actorPrefab)` with no
position, so all sixteen pre-filled slot bodies (ServerPlayerSlotPool, phase 3A) sit at
the prefab's authored spot near (0, 1000, 0). AnnounceNewActors announces them THERE,
at startup, before any of them is claimed. When one is later claimed and placed at a
real spawn point, MarkSpawnSent has already fired and the truth is never re-sent.

So every client holds a wrong position for every unclaimed slot body, permanently, and
for any actor outside interest range that wrong position is the only one it has.

The OnSpawn placement is still correct and is retained: it is right for a genuinely
fresh spawn, and it is a precondition for any of the candidate fixes to the second
defect. It is not sufficient on its own.


NO UNIT TEST EXISTS FOR THIS
----------------------------
Net/Client has no asmdef, so RemoteActorRegistry compiles into Assembly-CSharp, which
no test assembly can reference. Verification cost a full three-client lane-B run —
minutes, where a test would have been seconds. This is a dated, concrete instance of
the cost E-11b describes, and it is recorded on that row.
