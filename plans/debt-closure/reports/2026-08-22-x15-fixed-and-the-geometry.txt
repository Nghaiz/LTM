NetMovementAgent -- that is authored on Player Fps Actor.prefab and nowhere else -- so
ServerPlayer.Tick took its DETACHED branch permanently and the session MoveState free-fell.

uses that prefab and is driven by AiActorController, so authoring it would put a second
driver on every character in the game.

branch is documented as covering a body that has not spawned YET; nothing checked that the
'yet' ever ended, and for every networked player it never did.
